About Us

From Irpin to the Global Electronic Scene

SunFixer & OVM are Ukrainian electronic music producers and creators based in Irpin, Kiev Oblast. We specialize in dark, intricate electronic music that spans multiple genres including Dark Drum & Bass, Neurofunk, and Breakbeat.

Our sound is characterized by complex basslines, sharp synths, and futuristic sound design that reflects the Ukrainian electronic music scene's demanding standards and technical excellence.

Through our label CORE64 Records, we continue the legacy of early digital music production while pushing the boundaries of modern electronic music.

Legacy Meets Innovation

CORE64 Records draws inspiration from the legendary era of analog synthesizers and 8-bit Commodore C64 computers, representing the evolution from vintage to modern Core 64 CPUs used in electronic music production.

Our aesthetic blends elements of new wave and EDM disco with deep and darker instrumental songs, always incorporating 808 sounds with inspiration from the 1980s.

Founded on the legacy of early digital music production, from Atari ST/E and MEGA ST with Steinberg Pro24 DAW software era, we continue to push the boundaries of electronic music.
